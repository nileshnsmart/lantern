Start with your voice © 2026 Lantern · Made on Earth, with love

Stories start with a voice.

1. Speak. Tap the microphone and talk. Who is telling and who it is for are optional. Lantern asks gently, out loud, in their language, or simply records while family asks.

2. Explore. Lantern researches the history around the story as they talk, and the family checks every line together.

3. Create. Keep the approved story as a storybook (PDF) or a narrated short film (MP4) that ends in their own voice.

Each session has 10,000 tokens. Lantern never copies a voice or animates a real face.
